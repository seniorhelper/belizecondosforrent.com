About Belize Condos for Rent

Updated November 10, 2026

Renting a condo in Belize for a season raises questions a vacation search doesn't: which zone fits your budget, what a lease usually includes, who pays electricity and how to avoid rental scams. This site answers those, with an estimator to sketch a monthly budget and a request form if you'd like help finding a unit.

Who's behind it

The site is published by Eye To Ad Media (Denver, Colorado), an independent web publisher run by Zach Wennstedt. We aren't a brokerage or a property management company, and we don't hold deposits. If you ask for help, we may connect you with landlords or managers who have suitable units.

Our ground rules

  • Rent figures are guidance, not quotes. They vary by season, building and lease length; confirm with the landlord.
  • No fake listings or reviews.
  • Safety first. We'd rather you lose a unit than lose a deposit: verify the owner, see the condo (in person or live video) and get the lease in writing.

How it's paid for

Landlords can buy labeled featured spots, we may show ads, and an Expedia stay search may pay us a commission if you book a short stay through it. Read the affiliate disclosure.
