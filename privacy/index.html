Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers belizecondosforrent.com, a guide to renting a condo in Belize by the month or season, with a cost estimator and a free request form. It explains what we collect and who else may set cookies on the site.

When you send a condo request

The form collects what you choose to tell us, such as name, email, phone, move-in date, budget, preferred area and notes. It travels to the lead server run by our publisher, Eye To Ad Media (clients.eyetoad.com), and then to our inbox. We use it to answer you and to look for matching condos; with that purpose only, we may share your request with a landlord or property manager who may have a fit. We never sell it.

The cost estimator runs in your browser. The numbers you enter aren't sent to us unless you also submit the form.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 uses cookies to count visits and show us which rental zones and tools people use (pages, device, approximate region, referring site). We look at those patterns, not at individuals.

Expedia stay search (affiliate)

We include a stay search from Expedia Group's affiliate program for renters who want a few nights in an area before signing a lease. Using it may let Expedia and its tracking partners set cookies that credit this site, and if you book, we may get a commission. It costs you nothing extra. Your search and booking are covered by Expedia's privacy statement.

Ads

We may run Google AdSense ads. Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info, and read how Google uses partner-site data. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland get a consent choice. Paid landlord listings are labeled "Featured".

Your options

Block or clear cookies in your browser at any time. To view, change or remove a request you sent, reach us via the contact page.

Other

Not intended for children under 13. Updates to this policy change the date above.
